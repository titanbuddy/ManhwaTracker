Manhwa Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Manhwa Tracker is a browser extension that remembers which chapter of a manhwa, manga or webtoon you are on. This page explains what it stores and where.

What the extension stores

When you stay on a page that looks like a chapter, the extension saves a record containing the series name, the website, the chapter number, the page address, a cover image address (if the page provides one) and the time you read it. It also saves your settings (blocked and allowed sites, alert and tracking switches).

This data is stored in your browser using the extension storage area. It stays on your device unless you turn on cloud sync.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not record pages that are not chapter pages.
  • It does not read what you type, your passwords, or the content of the pages you visit beyond the page address, title and cover image tag.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not sell or share your data.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you create an account and sign in, your library records (as described above) are uploaded to a database hosted by Supabase so that your progress can be shared across your devices. Your email address and a password hash are held by the authentication service for the account. Database access rules restrict each account to its own records. Signing out stops syncing; to delete your cloud data, contact the address below.

New-chapter alerts (optional)

If you turn on alerts, the extension asks for permission to access websites. It then fetches the series pages you have already read, periodically, to look for newer chapter links. The result is stored on your device only. Requests are sent without cookies. You can turn alerts off at any time and revoke the permission in your browser's extension settings.

Controls

You can pause tracking, block specific sites, restrict tracking to an allowlist, remove any series, and export your data as a file. Uninstalling the extension deletes the data stored on your device.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and significant changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
